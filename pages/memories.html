Har Yaad Aaj Bhi Yaad Hai...

Aap ke saath guzara hua har lamha... Aaj bhi meri zindagi ki sab se khoobsurat yaadon mein se ek hai. Aap ki smile... Aap ki narazgi... Aap ki hasi... Sab kuch aaj bhi yaad hai. Main un yaadon ko kabhi bhool nahi saka. Aur shayad kabhi bhool bhi nahi paunga. Main sirf relationship ko miss nahi karta... Main aap ko miss karta hun. Aur us sukoon ko bhi... Jo sirf aap ke saath mehsoos hota tha. Aaj bhi meri dua sirf itni hai... Allah hamesha aap ko khush rakhe. Aur agar meri qismat mein dobara aap ka saath likha ho... To main is baar us rishte ki qadar pehle se kahin zyada karunga. ❤️
