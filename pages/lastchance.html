Bas Itni Si Darkhwast...

Agar aap yahan tak aa gayi hain... To dil se shukriya. Main janta hun ke sirf words se trust wapas nahi aata. Trust hamesha actions se banta hai. Aur agar kabhi Allah ne dobara humein ek mauqa diya... To main har din apni actions se ye sabit karunga ke main waqai badal gaya hun. Main phir kabhi aap ko control karne ki koshish nahi karunga. Main phir kabhi aap ki feelings ko ignore nahi karunga. Main phir kabhi gusse ko mohabbat se upar nahi rakhunga. Main sirf itna chahta hun... Ke agar aap ke dil mein mere liye ab bhi thodi si bhi jagah baqi ho... Aur agar aap munasib samjhein... To mujhe ek akhri mauqa de dein. Main wada nahi karunga ke humari zindagi mein kabhi problems nahi aayengi... Lekin ye zaroor wada karta hun ke un problems ke khilaf main aur aap ek team honge... Ek dusre ke khilaf nahi. ❤️
